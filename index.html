How it works

Three things a good night dispatcher does. Done the same way at every shop.

01 — INTERCEPT

The after-hours line forwards to Keystone.

A call-forwarding change at each shop, nothing installed. Residential misdials and sales calls are filtered out before they cost anyone sleep.

No migration. No new software at the shop.
02 — TRIAGE

It asks what a dispatcher would ask.

Which site. What failed. What's at risk. Who's on-site. It already has the facility's notes from the last visit in front of it, so it knows a chiller trip at a food plant is not a thermostat call.

Industrial failures first. Everything else logged for morning.
03 — DISPATCH

It authorizes the fee and pages the tech.

Your emergency call-out rate, spoken and confirmed on the call. The on-call technician gets the page with the site notes attached, and the ticket is written up for the shop's dispatch board.

Under a minute, ring to page.
